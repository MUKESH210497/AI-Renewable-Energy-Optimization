## Abstract — Draft
Renewable energy organizations face uncertainty in generation and operational planning. This research proposes to evaluate the opportunities and barriers of AI-based forecasting and automation using a management survey and an empirical solar power forecasting exercise. The study will compare a persistence baseline with Random Forest and Gradient Boosting on held-out time-series data, alongside descriptive analysis of adoption-related survey responses. Findings, conclusions and recommendations will be completed after real data collection and analysis.

## Chapter 1 — Introduction
### Background
Solar and wind operations depend on environmental conditions and asset performance. Data-driven methods may support forecasting, anomaly identification and operational planning. Practical value depends on reliable data, integration, costs and decision processes.
### Problem
