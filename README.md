# AI-Driven Operational Efficiency in the Renewable Energy Sector

MBA final-year research proposal on predictive analytics, automation, and sustainable business performance in renewable energy.

**Status:** Proposal and research planning only. No empirical findings, trained models, or dashboards are claimed.

## Scope
- Solar generation forecasting compared with a baseline
- AI adoption and perceived operational efficiency
- Predictive maintenance opportunities and barriers
- Evidence-based recommendations

## Research ethics
Use voluntary informed consent, protect respondent privacy, and do not upload confidential employer or client data.
