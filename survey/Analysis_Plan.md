# Survey and Statistical Analysis Plan

## Study design
Cross-sectional voluntary survey of professionals relevant to renewable energy operations and analytics. Proposed recruitment target: 60–100 if feasible; report achieved sample, missingness and eligibility.

## Constructs
- Adoption: AI usage frequency and AI use cases.
- Perceived operational efficiency: survey agreement items, if a valid multi-item scale is developed.
- Decision effectiveness: perceived value of predictive analytics.
- Data readiness: accessibility and quality perceptions.
- Barriers: perceived cost, skills and integration issues.

## Measurement cautions
The current questionnaire has mostly one item per construct. Do not report Cronbach's alpha for a one-item scale. Expand and pilot multi-item constructs before estimating scale reliability or regression models. A respondent without direct sector experience must be separately categorized.

## Analysis workflow
1. Confirm informed consent and eligibility.
2. Remove direct identifiers; retain only permitted anonymized data.
3. Record response counts and missingness by question.
4. Produce frequency tables and descriptive statistics.
5. For ordered Likert items, report distributions and medians; justify any numeric treatment.
6. If a sufficient, appropriate sample exists, run exploratory Spearman associations.
7. Use multivariable regression only with justified sample size, assumptions and transparent reporting.
8. Discuss selection bias, common-method bias, confounding and inability to infer causation.

## Hypotheses
H1: Adoption associated with perceived efficiency.
H2: Analytics usage associated with perceived decision effectiveness.
H3: Data readiness associated with implementation success.
H4: Costs and skill gaps negatively associated with adoption.

**No hypothesis has been tested.** The current questionnaire may need additional validated items to operationalize these fully.

## Data handling
Keep raw responses outside the public repository. Publish aggregate tables only after checking for small-cell disclosure and obtaining appropriate consent.
